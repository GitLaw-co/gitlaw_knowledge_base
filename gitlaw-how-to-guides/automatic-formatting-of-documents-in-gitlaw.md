#### Sections

GitLaw automatically marks up documents with Sections for fast navigation. To jump between sections, open the **…** menu next to the document title and choose **Document contents**.

![The Document contents panel listing the document's sections](https://git.law/kb-images/automatic-formatting-of-documents-in-gitlaw/document-contents.png "narrow; ring: 7 19 92 79")

#### Clauses

GitLaw builds the clause list from your headings. Apply Heading 1, 2, 3 and so on in the editor, and the clause structure updates automatically.
