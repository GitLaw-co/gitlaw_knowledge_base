GitLaw has three tiers: **Free**, **Pro**, and **Enterprise**. An organisation can mix free and paid members - you pay only for the members who need Pro. Sharing documents with people outside your organisation is always free. The AI runs on **AI credits** - a small amount is used each time you draft or review a document.

## The three tiers

|                        | Free                             | Pro                                          | Enterprise              |
| ---------------------- | -------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------- | ----------------------- |
| Price                  | $0/month              | $25/month per seat (or $20/month per seat billed annually) | Custom — contact us     |
| Monthly AI credits     | $5 of credits/month | $25 of credits per seat/month     | Custom credit pool      |
| Members                | Unlimited                        | Unlimited                                    | Unlimited               |
| Documents              | Unlimited                        | Unlimited                                    | Unlimited               |
| Advanced team settings | —                                | Yes                                          | Yes                     |
| Audit log & versions   | —                                | Yes                                          | Yes, extended retention |
| Security               | Standard                         | Standard                                     | SSO / SAML              |
| Support                | Community                        | Priority                                     | Dedicated support + SLA |

**Free** is a real tier, not a trial. Unlimited members, unlimited documents, and a monthly AI-credit allowance - for a solo user or a whole team.

**Pro** is the paid self-serve tier, priced **per seat**. Each seat gets more AI credits, plus advanced team settings, audit log and version history, and priority support. Scale up by adding seats or buying top-ups.

**Enterprise** is sales-led and custom-priced. It adds a custom credit pool, SSO/SAML, dedicated support with an SLA, and extended audit retention. [Contact us](mailto:hey@git.law).

## What are AI credits?

**AI credits** power GitLaw's AI - drafting contracts, reviewing documents. Each use takes a small amount of credits, based on how much work is involved.

### What affects the cost?

- **Document length** - longer contracts take more processing
- **Amount of context** - the more documents GitLaw compares or references, the more credits are used
- **Number of revisions** - the AI runs several cycles to get a good result (this is why a document often takes 1-3 minutes)

### How much does a document typically cost?

| Scenario                  | Typical credit cost |
| ------------------------- | ------------------- |
| Standard document         | $0.05 – $0.50       |
| Large or complex document | $1 – $2 (rare)      |

Most documents sit in the standard range.

## How credits are allocated

Each seat gets a **monthly allowance** that resets every billing cycle. Allowances don't transfer between members - each person has their own.

If someone runs out, you have two options:

- **Upgrade** from Free to Pro for a larger allowance per seat, or
- **Buy a top-up** - extra credits your Paid Members share.

Top-ups are pooled at the organisation level. They kick in only after a Paid Member's own monthly allowance runs out - Free Members use only their own allowance - and - unlike the monthly allowance - **they roll over and don't expire**. See [AI credits and top-ups](/knowledge-base/ai-credits-and-top-ups).

## Sharing is always free

Anyone you share a file, folder, or Project with works from their **own** GitLaw organisation, using their own credits. They're never billed to you - on any tier, including Free. See [Do I pay for people I share documents with?](/knowledge-base/do-i-pay-for-people-i-share-with).

## Already on the old $20/month plan?

You keep your $20/month rate on Pro, with the same features as new Pro members - no forced price rise. See [I'm on the old $20/month plan — what changes?](/knowledge-base/old-20-plan-what-changes).
