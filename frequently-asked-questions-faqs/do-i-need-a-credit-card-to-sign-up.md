No. You can sign up for GitLaw and start working straight away - no credit card, no trial period, no hidden commitment.

## What you get for free

Every free account includes:

- **$5 of AI credits every month** - enough to draft and review most standard contracts
- **Unlimited documents** - no cap on how many you store or create
- **AI drafting and review** - the same AI features available on paid plans
- **E-signature** - send documents for signing at no extra cost

The free plan is not a watered-down trial. You get the same core features as paid users - the main difference is the monthly credit allowance. See [How does billing work?](/knowledge-base/how-does-billing-work) for a full plan comparison.

## When would I need to add payment details?

Only if you choose to upgrade to **Pro** or buy a credit top-up mid-month. Until then, no payment information is needed.

## What happens if I run out of free credits?

AI features (drafting and review) pause until your credits reset at the start of the next billing cycle. Your documents remain fully accessible and you can continue using GitLaw's non-AI features.
