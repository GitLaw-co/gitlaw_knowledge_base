Cancelling your plan keeps your account and every document. Deleting your account removes your private documents for good, so export them first.

- **Keep my account:** [cancel or pause my plan](#cancelling-or-pausing-your-plan). You move to the free plan and nothing is deleted.
- **Leave GitLaw:** [delete my account](#deleting-your-account). Private documents can't be recovered.

## Cancelling or pausing your plan

1. Click **Credits** at the bottom of the left sidebar, then **Billing details**.

   ![The Credits menu with Billing details](https://git.law/kb-images/what-happens-to-my-documents-if-i-cancel-or-delete-my-account/credits-menu.png "narrow; ring: 3 59 94 19")

2. On the Billing page, click **Cancel plan** on your plan card. On the free plan there's nothing to cancel, so you only see **View Plans**.

   ![The plan card on the Billing page](https://git.law/kb-images/what-happens-to-my-documents-if-i-cancel-or-delete-my-account/billing.png "ring: 89.4 28 9.4 44")

3. GitLaw offers to **pause** your plan instead. Pausing stops charges and freezes your credit balance until you resume. To cancel anyway, confirm with **Cancel and lose…**

When you cancel, your plan runs until the end of the period you paid for, then you move to the free plan. Unused plan credits are lost when the plan ends. Documents, folders and shared files stay exactly where they are, and you can export any document on any plan. See [How do I cancel or change my plan?](/knowledge-base/how-do-i-cancel-or-change-my-plan)

## Deleting your account

There is no delete button in the app yet. You request deletion by email, and we do it for you. **Deletion can't be undone**, so export every document you want to keep first.

1. Open each document you want to keep, click the **download** icon at the top right, and choose **Download as Word** or **Download as PDF**. See [Export your document](/knowledge-base/export-your-document).

   ![The download menu with Download as Word and Download as PDF](https://git.law/kb-images/what-happens-to-my-documents-if-i-cancel-or-delete-my-account/download-menu.png "ring: 21.5 27 50.5 66")

2. Check which email your account uses: open the menu under your name at the bottom left, then **Account Settings**. Your email is listed under **Account emails**.

   ![The account menu with Account Settings](https://git.law/kb-images/what-happens-to-my-documents-if-i-cancel-or-delete-my-account/account-menu.png "narrow; ring: 4 50 92 11")

   ![Account Settings with the Account emails section](https://git.law/kb-images/what-happens-to-my-documents-if-i-cancel-or-delete-my-account/account-settings.png "ring: 0.5 63 99 35")

3. From that email address, write to privacy@git.law and ask us to delete your account. We may ask you to confirm it's you before we go ahead.

## What happens to your documents

| Document                             | After deletion                                                           |
| ------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Private, with no collaborators       | **Deleted** with your account                                            |
| Shared with collaborators, or public | **Kept**: moved to GitLaw's organisation so collaborators keep access    |
| Belongs to an organisation           | **Kept**: stays with the organisation, members keep the same permissions |
| Your profile and settings            | **Deleted**                                                              |

## Frequently asked questions

### Will I lose my documents if I cancel my plan?

No. Cancelling only changes your plan. Your account and documents stay as they are.

### Is my data used to train AI?

No. Your documents aren't used to train AI models, by GitLaw or by its AI providers. See [Is my data used to train AI?](/knowledge-base/is-my-data-used-to-train-ai)
