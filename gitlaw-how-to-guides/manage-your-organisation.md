A GitLaw organisation is a shared workspace for your team. Documents created inside an organisation workspace are owned by the organisation - not the individual who created them - so your firm's work stays with the firm even when people move on.

## Create an organisation

1. Open your account menu and select **New organisation**.
2. Enter a name and confirm.
3. You are automatically assigned the **Org Owner** role.

## Roles at a glance

| Role       | Sees all org files | Invites members | Removes members  | Changes roles          | Edits org settings |
| ---------- | ------------------ | --------------- | ---------------- | ---------------------- | ------------------ |
| **Owner**  | No (unless shared) | Yes             | Yes              | Owner / Admin / Member | Yes                |
| **Admin**  | No (unless shared) | Yes             | Yes (not Owners) | Admin / Member only    | No                 |
| **Member** | No (unless shared) | No              | No               | No                     | No                 |

There must always be at least one Owner. An Owner can only leave or remove their own Owner role if another Owner already exists.

For full details on document-level access (Viewer, Editor, Owner), see [File sharing permissions in personal and organisation accounts](/knowledge-base/file-sharing-permissions-in-personal-and-organisation-accounts).

## Invite members

1. Go to your organisation's **Members** settings.
2. Enter the person's email address and select a role.
3. They receive an invitation to join. Once accepted, they can create and collaborate on organisation documents.

You can invite as many members as you like. You pay only for Paid Members; Free Members cost you nothing. See [How does billing work?](/knowledge-base/how-does-billing-work) for plan details.

## Change a member's role

1. Open **Members** settings.
2. Find the member and select a new role from the dropdown.

Owners can change any role. Admins can change Member and Admin roles but cannot grant or revoke the Owner role.

## AI credits and top-ups

Each member has their own monthly AI allowance. Top-ups you buy go into a shared pool that Paid Members draw on once their own allowance runs out. Free Members use only their own allowance. Owners and Admins can monitor usage and buy top-ups from your organisation settings. See [AI credits and top-ups](/knowledge-base/ai-credits-and-top-ups).

## Remove a member (off-boarding)

1. Open **Members** settings.
2. Select the member and choose **Remove from organisation**.

When a member leaves or is removed:

- **Organisation-owned documents** stay with the organisation and remain accessible to Owners and anyone they were shared with.
- **Personally-owned documents** stay with the departing user and are not accessible to the organisation.
- Access is revoked immediately.

To keep work within the organisation, make sure documents are created inside the organisation workspace - or transfer ownership before the person leaves.

## Members leaving voluntarily

Any member can leave an organisation at any time from their account settings. The same ownership rules apply as above.
