You pay for **Paid Members** of your own organisation. Two things never touch your bill: **Free Members**, and people at other organisations you share documents with.

## Two kinds of member

Everyone in your organisation is a full member of the team. The difference is who funds their seat.

- **Paid Member** - a seat your organisation pays for. This is what "billable seat" means.
- **Free Member** - a full member who costs you nothing. They use the free AI allowance funded by GitLaw, and can't use your organisation's top-up credits. Good for part-time collaborators, contractors, or family who don't need a paid seat.

Both are proper members with access to your shared documents. You just only pay for the Paid ones.

## People you share with are not members

Someone at another organisation you share a file, folder, or Project with is **not** a member of your organisation - so they're never a billable seat. They work from their own GitLaw organisation, with their own credits. See [Do I pay for people I share documents with?](/knowledge-base/do-i-pay-for-people-i-share-with).

## Who counts - at a glance

| Person                                | Member of your org? | Billed to you? |
| ------------------------------------- | ------------------- | -------------- |
| A Paid Member                         | Yes                 | Yes            |
| A Free Member                         | Yes                 | No             |
| Someone at another org you share with | No                  | No             |

## The short version

Your bill = the number of **Paid Members** in your organisation. Everyone else is free. For how seats and pricing fit together, see [How does billing work?](/knowledge-base/how-does-billing-work).
