No. A colleague you invite only sees the organisation you invited them to. Inside it, they see what is shared with everyone, never what you keep to yourself.

## Your worlds are kept separate

Each **organisation** has its own contacts and its own **memory** (the things GitLaw remembers about how you work). They never mix.

- Your personal organisation holds personal contacts - your landlord, your plumber, your dentist.
- Your business organisation holds business contacts.

Invite a colleague into your business organisation and they never see your personal organisation.

## Only me, or everyone

Inside an organisation, every contact and memory note has a **Who sees it** setting:

- **Only me** - only you see it. It carries an **Only you** badge.
- **Everyone in the organization** - every member sees it.

New contacts are shared with everyone unless you choose **Only me**; new memory notes start as **Only me**. You can change the setting later either way. Contacts and notes GitLaw finds in an uploaded file follow that file's visibility.

## Who can change what

| Role   | Contacts and memory everyone sees                          | Their own Only me ones |
| ------ | ---------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------- |
| Owner  | Can change or delete any of them                           | Can change or delete   |
| Admin  | Can change or delete any of them                           | Can change or delete   |
| Member | Can add new ones, and change or delete the ones they added | Can change or delete   |

Nobody can open another member's **Only me** contacts or notes - owners and admins included.

## What the Agent saves

The Agent saves what it learns to your **Only me** memory as it works, without asking first. It only writes to shared memory when an owner or admin asks it to. Contacts it saves are shared with everyone. Open **Memory** to see, edit or delete what it saved.

## When someone leaves

The member leaving, or whoever removes them, chooses what happens to the files, contacts and notes they kept as **Only me**: **Hand over** to an owner who stays, or **Delete them**. A file the member shared with someone is never deleted - it goes to the owner, so the people it was shared with keep it. An admin who removes a member can only hand over. What everyone sees stays with the organisation.

If an owner deletes the organisation, **Move it to the people it belongs to** returns each member's private files, contacts and notes; **Delete everything** deletes them too.

## What about your own preferences?

Your personal preferences - tone of voice, "no emoji", language, theme - are never stored in memory, so they are never shared into a team's memory.

Short version: invite freely, and keep anything personal as **Only me**.
