Only the shared ones. Every file in GitLaw is either **shared with your organisation** or **private** - you choose. Teammates see the shared files; private files stay yours.

## You choose as you go

When you add a file - upload it, draft it, or sync it in - you choose whether it's shared with your organisation or private. You can change it later.

- **Shared with the organisation** - everyone in your organisation can see it.
- **Private** - only you can open it.

## Seeing isn't editing

Seeing a shared file doesn't mean changing it. You choose whether someone can **view** or **edit** - editing always needs a grant.

## What happens when you invite someone

A new member sees the files shared with the organisation - not your private ones. Inviting someone never exposes a private file.

## If you're working solo

While it's just you, the choice doesn't show - nobody else is there to see anything. It starts to matter the moment you invite someone.

## One thing to know

A document is private to the person who created it until it is shared - and that applies to **Owners** and **Admins** too. Being an Owner does not grant access to other members' private documents.
