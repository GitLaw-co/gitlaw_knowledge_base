Not yet. GitLaw doesn't have a Teams feature - there's no way to create a named group of people and manage their document access as a group.

Here's what you can do instead:

- **Share with individual people.** Add anyone to a document by email or share link and choose the access level you give them.
- **Use an organisation account.** An [organisation](/knowledge-base/manage-your-organisation) is a shared workspace where members have access to the organisation's documents.

## Want Teams in GitLaw?

We prioritise what people ask for. If team-based access groups would help you, email us at [hey@git.law](mailto:hey@git.law) - the more requests we get, the sooner it moves up the list.
