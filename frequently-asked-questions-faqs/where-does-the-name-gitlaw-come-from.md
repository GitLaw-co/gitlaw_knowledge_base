---
title: Where does the name GitLaw come from?
slug: where-does-the-name-gitlaw-come-from
category: frequently-asked-questions-faqs
excerpt: GitLaw takes its name from Git, the version control system developers use to track changes and collaborate - applied to legal documents.
---

GitLaw is inspired by the term "Git," which is a popular version control system used by developers to manage code. Just like Git helps developers track changes, collaborate, and manage versions of their projects, GitLaw is designed to help legal professionals manage legal documents, track revisions, and collaborate efficiently. The name reflects our commitment to bringing the same level of organization, control, and collaboration to the legal field.
