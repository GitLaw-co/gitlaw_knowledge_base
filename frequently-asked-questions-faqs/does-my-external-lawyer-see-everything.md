It depends how you bring them in. There are two ways, and they see different amounts.

## Option 1: share a Project

A **Project** is a shared space for a specific deal or matter. Share one and your lawyer sees only what's inside it - nothing else in your organisation.

You choose the scope:

| Scope       | What they see                                                                               |
| ----------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Bounded** | Only that Project's files and contacts                                                      |
| **Broad**   | Also your organisation **Profile** and **Playbooks** (your saved preferences and positions) |

Default to **bounded** unless you mean to share more. They work from their own GitLaw organisation, they're never billed to you, and their access begins and ends with the Project.

## Option 2: invite them into your organisation

Invite your lawyer or accountant as a member and they're on the team - they see the files **shared with your organisation**, like any other member. Private files stay private unless you share them directly.

## Which to use?

For a single deal or matter, share a Project - access begins and ends with the deal. For an adviser who works with you all the time, invite them in - and keep sensitive files private.

## Revoking access

Shared a Project? Remove them from it. Invited them in? Remove them as a member.
