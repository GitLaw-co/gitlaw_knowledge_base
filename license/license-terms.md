Choose from four streamlined Creative Commons license options that address the majority of legal content sharing needs. For situations requiring different terms, select "Custom License" to provide your own licensing agreement."

**Want maximum adoption?** → Choose **CC BY 4.0**

**Want to force derivatives to be open?** → Choose **CC BY-SA 4.0**

**Want to prevent all changes?** → Choose **CC BY-ND 4.0**

**Want no restrictions at all?** → Choose **CC0**

**Have different requirements?** → Choose **Custom License**

## Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 (GitLaw Default)

**CC BY 4.0**

**Best for:** Legal templates, forms, guides, and any content you want widely shared

**What it allows:**

- ✅ Commercial use
- ✅ Modifications
- ✅ Distribution
- ✅ Private use

**Requirements:**

- ⚠️ Attribution required

**Summary:** The most permissive license available. Others can use, modify, distribute, and even sell your work as long as they give you credit. Perfect for maximizing adoption of your legal resources.

[View Full License →](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)

## Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International

**CC BY-SA 4.0**

**Best for:** Legal resources you want to keep freely available

**What it allows:**

- ✅ Commercial use
- ✅ Modifications
- ✅ Distribution
- ✅ Private use

**Requirements:**

- ⚠️ Attribution required
- ⚠️ Derivative works must use same license

**Summary:** Ensures your work stays open. Anyone can use and modify your work, but they must share improvements under the same license. Prevents your open work from being made proprietary.

[View Full License →](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/)

## Creative Commons Attribution-NoDerivatives 4.0 International

**CC BY-ND 4.0**

**Best for:** Legal documents that must remain unchanged

**What it allows:**

- ✅ Commercial use
- ✅ Distribution
- ✅ Private use

**What it prohibits:**

- ❌ Modifications not allowed

**Requirements:**

- ⚠️ Attribution required

**Summary:** Others can share and use your work commercially, but cannot change it. Perfect for official forms, policies, or standardized documents where accuracy is critical.

**Used by:** Some Atlassian Marketplace agreements

[View Full License →](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nd/4.0/)

## Creative Commons Zero

**CC0 1.0 Universal**

**Best for:** Public benefit legal resources

**What it allows:**

- ✅ Commercial use
- ✅ Modifications
- ✅ Distribution
- ✅ Private use
- ✅ No attribution required

**Summary:** Dedicates your work to the public domain. No restrictions, no requirements. Ideal for basic legal forms or resources you want to contribute freely to the legal community.

[View Full License →](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/)

## Custom License

**For specialized needs:** Upload your own license file if none of the Creative Commons options work for your situation.

**Common reasons:**

- Bar association requirements
- Institutional policies
- Client-specific terms
- Proprietary but limited sharing

_This guide provides general information only and does not constitute legal advice. Consult with an attorney if you need specific guidance about licensing your work._
