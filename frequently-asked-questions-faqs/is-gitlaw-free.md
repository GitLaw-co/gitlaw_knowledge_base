Yes - GitLaw has a genuinely free plan, and you don't need a credit card to get started.

## What's included on the free plan

- **Unlimited documents** - create and store as many contracts as you like
- **AI drafting** - generate contract drafts using AI
- **AI contract review** - get AI-powered analysis of any document
- **Free templates** - the full template library is free, always
- **E-signatures** - send and sign documents electronically at no cost
- **$5 of AI credits every month** - enough to draft or review several standard documents

The free plan is a genuine starting point, not a trial. There's no time limit and no card required.

## What do paid plans add?

The main difference is how many AI credits you get each month; paid tiers also add team and support features:

| Plan       | Price                                        | Monthly AI credits                       |
| ---------- | -------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- |
| Free       | $0                                | $5 of credits/month         |
| Pro        | $25/month per seat (or $20/month per seat billed annually) | $25 of credits per seat/month |
| Enterprise | Custom — contact us                          | Custom credit pool                       |

**Pro** also adds advanced team settings, audit log and version history, and priority support. **Enterprise** adds SSO/SAML, extended audit retention, and dedicated support with an SLA. Every feature - AI drafting, AI review, e-signatures, templates - is available on all plans.

For full pricing details, credit costs, and how the organisation credit pool works, see [How does billing work?](/knowledge-base/how-does-billing-work)
