title: What can an AI assistant connected to GitLaw access?

excerpt: An AI assistant connected to GitLaw acts as you. It sees only what you can see, every change it makes is tracked, and you can revoke it at any time.
---

When you connect Claude, ChatGPT or another AI assistant to GitLaw, it signs in as you. It doesn't get a separate account or any extra access.

### What it can see

- **Only what you can see.** Your assistant can search and read the documents your GitLaw account can open, and nothing more. Documents shared with you follow the same permissions they have in GitLaw.

### What it can change

- **Every change is tracked.** Edits are saved as new versions with full history, and can be recorded as tracked changes you accept or reject in GitLaw.
- **It can't sign or send.** Sending for signature and sharing with the other party happen in GitLaw, by you.

### Your data

Connecting an assistant doesn't change how GitLaw handles your documents, and they are not used to train AI models. See [Is my data used to train AI?](/knowledge-base/is-my-data-used-to-train-ai). What your assistant keeps from the chat itself is covered by your Claude or ChatGPT plan's privacy settings.

### How to disconnect

Go to **Settings → Connected apps** and click **Revoke** next to the app. It loses access straight away. Each connection is listed separately, so revoke all of them if you connected more than once.
