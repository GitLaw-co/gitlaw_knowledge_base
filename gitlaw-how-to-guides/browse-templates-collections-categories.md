GitLaw's template library is large, so it's organised with **collections** and **categories**. Together they make it much faster to find the right contract instead of scrolling through everything.

## Collections vs categories

Both help you navigate the library, but in different ways:

|                | Collections                                       | Categories                                        |
| -------------- | ------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------- |
| What it is     | A curated grouping of related templates           | A classification by document type or subject area |
| Think of it as | A themed shelf someone has put together           | A label that says what kind of document this is   |
| Use it to      | Explore a set of templates grouped around a theme | Narrow the library down to one type of document   |

A single template can sit in a category (telling you what it is) and also appear in one or more collections (grouping it with related documents).

## How to browse and filter

1. **Open the template library:** click **Templates** in the left sidebar. The **Overview** tab shows popular categories and a search box for all templates.

   ![The Templates overview with search and popular categories](https://git.law/kb-images/browse-templates-collections-categories/templates-overview.png "ring: 23.8 33.5 52 12")

2. **Browse by category:** open the **Template Library** tab and pick a category from the row at the top - for example Employment, IP, or Service Agreements.

   ![The Template Library tab with the category row and search](https://git.law/kb-images/browse-templates-collections-categories/templates-library.png "ring: 0 24 100 17")

3. **Explore collections** to discover curated groups of related templates you might not have searched for directly.
4. **Combine with search and jurisdiction filters** to narrow results further. Click the filter icon next to the search box and choose **Jurisdiction** so you only see documents relevant to where you operate.

   ![The filter menu with Jurisdiction](https://git.law/kb-images/browse-templates-collections-categories/templates-filter.png "ring: 1.5 29 67 67")

## Why organising helps you find contracts faster

- **Less scrolling** - categories cut the library down to the kind of document you actually need.
- **Better discovery** - collections surface related templates you may not have thought to look for.
- **Cleaner results** - combining categories, collections, and jurisdiction filters gets you to the right starting point quickly.

For more on how templates are sourced and which ones are verified, see [Who writes GitLaw's templates and can I trust them?](/knowledge-base/who-writes-gitlaw-templates).
