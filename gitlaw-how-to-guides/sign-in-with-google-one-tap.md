GitLaw supports **Google One Tap**, a faster way to sign in using your Google account. Instead of filling in a form, you can get into GitLaw with a single tap.

## What One Tap is

One Tap is a sign-in prompt from Google that can appear automatically when you visit GitLaw in a browser where you're already logged in to Google. It shows the Google account you're signed in with and lets you continue to GitLaw in one click - no password to type.

## How it appears

When you're signed in to Google in your browser, a small One Tap prompt may appear near the top of the page showing your Google account. Select your account to sign in.

If the prompt doesn't appear, you can still sign in the usual way - including the standard "Continue with Google" option, which uses the same account.

## It's the same account

One Tap signs you in to the **same GitLaw account** as the normal Google sign-in. There's no separate account to set up: whether you use One Tap or the regular Google button, you end up in the same place with the same documents and settings.

## Privacy and safety

- One Tap only works when you're already signed in to Google in that browser.
- It shows you which Google account will be used before you confirm - you stay in control of which account signs in.
- You can dismiss the prompt and sign in another way at any time.
- On a shared or public computer, sign in deliberately and remember to sign out of both GitLaw and Google when you're done.

## Quick reference

| Question                         | Answer                                                        |
| -------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------- |
| What does One Tap do?            | Signs you in to GitLaw in one click with your Google account. |
| Is it a different account?       | No - it's the same account as "Continue with Google".         |
| Do I need a password?            | No, One Tap skips the password step.                          |
| What if the prompt doesn't show? | Sign in the usual way, including "Continue with Google".      |
