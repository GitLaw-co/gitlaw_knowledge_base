GitLaw maintains a single privacy policy:

**[Read the GitLaw Privacy Policy](https://git.law/privacy-policy)**

It explains what Personal Data GitLaw collects, why we process it, who we share it with, how long we keep it, and the rights you have over it - including the rights that apply if you are in the EEA, the UK, or a US state with its own privacy law.
