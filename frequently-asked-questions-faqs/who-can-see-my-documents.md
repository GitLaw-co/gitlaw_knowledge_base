**Private by default - and you can always see who has access.**

Every document you create is **private by default**, whether you create it in your own space or inside an organisation. Nobody else can see it - including other members of your organisation - until you share it or change its visibility.

### How access is granted

You control access through two mechanisms:

- **Direct invitations** - share a document with a specific person and assign them a role: Viewer (read-only), Editor (can edit and reshare), or Owner (full control including deletion).
- **Shared links** - generate a link that anyone with it can open, or restrict the link to invited collaborators only. Links can be revoked at any time.

### Files inside an organisation

A document owned by an organisation is still private to you until you share it. Share it with the organisation when you want colleagues to find and collaborate on it.

### External parties

You can share documents with counterparties or clients outside your organisation. They only ever see the specific document you share - never your other files, your organisation, or its members.
