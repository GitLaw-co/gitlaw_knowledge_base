**Almost nothing changes for you.** If you're already paying $20/month, you keep that rate on the new **Pro** tier.

## What stays the same

- **Your price.** You stay on $20/month - no forced price rise.
- **Your features.** You get the full **Pro** feature set: more AI credits, advanced team settings, audit log and version history, priority support.
- **Your credits.** At switch-over, everyone gets a credit allowance matching their new tier - nobody starts worse off than before.

New signups pay the new Pro price of $25/month per seat. You don't.

## What happens if you add seats

Your original seat stays at $20/month. Additional seats are charged at the new Pro rate.

| Seat                             | Rate                |
| -------------------------------- | ------------------- |
| Your original grandfathered seat | $20/month |
| Each additional seat             | New Pro rate        |

Growing your team never costs you the grandfathered seat.

## Do I need to do anything?

No. The change happens automatically, and we'll email you the details. If anything looks off after the switch-over, [get in touch](mailto:hey@git.law) and we'll sort it out.
