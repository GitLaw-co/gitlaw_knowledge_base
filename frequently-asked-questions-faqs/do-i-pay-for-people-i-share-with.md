No. **Sharing is always free**, on every tier - including Free.

When you share a file or a folder, the other person opens it from their **own** GitLaw organisation, using their **own** AI credits. Nothing they do is billed to you.

## Why it's free

GitLaw only charges for the members of **your** organisation - the seats you pay for. Someone at another organisation you share a document with isn't a member of your team, so there's nothing to bill.

Send a contract to a client, a co-founder, or the other side's lawyer, and:

- **They** open it in their organisation.
- **They** use their own credits if they run the AI on it.
- **You** pay the same as before you shared it.

## A quick example

You're on Pro with three seats and share a draft agreement with a client for review.

| Who                                  | Billed to you? |
| ------------------------------------ | -------------- |
| Your three Pro seats                 | Yes            |
| The client you shared with           | No             |
| The client's colleagues they loop in | No             |

The client works in their own GitLaw organisation. If they don't have one yet, they can start on Free.

## What does count toward your bill

Only members of your own organisation - and only the paid ones. See [Who counts as a billable seat?](/knowledge-base/who-counts-as-a-billable-seat).
