The GitLaw Editor includes familiar text-editing controls, plus features designed for legal drafting. Here’s an overview of the main tools in the toolbar:

### Text Styles

Use the **H** drop-down near the left of the toolbar to change paragraph styles (e.g., normal text, Heading 1, Heading 2). Headings help structure your document and automatically generate a table of contents.

### Bold, Italics, Underline, and Strikethrough

Apply direct formatting using the **B**, **I**, **U**, and **S** icons. These work just like in standard word processors ✍️.

### Lists and Indentation

Click the **bulleted** or **numbered list** icons to organize content. Use **Increase indent** and **Decrease indent** in the toolbar's **…** menu to create nested list items.

- **Numbered lists** are ideal for clauses and sub-clauses.
- **Bulleted lists** work well for key points or optional terms.

![The editor toolbar: heading menu, lists, bold, italic, underline, strikethrough, alignment, table, smart field and more](https://git.law/kb-images/basic-formatting-options-in-gitlaw-editor/editor-toolbar.png "ring: 10.5 15 6.5 70; ring: 26 15 15.5 70")

### Links and Images

- To add a hyperlink: highlight the text, open the **…** menu at the right of the toolbar and choose **Insert link**.
- To add an image: choose **Insert image** from the toolbar's **…** menu, or drag-and-drop an image into the upload window.

👉 We accept JPG, PNG, and AVIF formats and a maximum size of 8 MB.

### Tables

Click the **table** icon and choose a grid size to insert a table.
Use the table's **three-dot menu** to merge cells, add or delete rows and columns.

👉 Formatting applied in the editor is preserved in the exported version. If you want to add help text, see advanced formatting options.

![The toolbar's … menu with Insert link, Insert image, Mark as help text and indentation](https://git.law/kb-images/basic-formatting-options-in-gitlaw-editor/toolbar-more.png "narrow; ring: 2 23 95 13.5")

GitLaw automatically applies **consistent numbering** and **section headings** when you create new clauses. Uploaded Word documents are converted to **markdown**, divided into sections, and formatted for easy clause-level editing.
