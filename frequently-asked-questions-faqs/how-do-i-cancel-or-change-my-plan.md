You can change or cancel your subscription yourself at any time from your billing settings. No support ticket required.

## Open your billing settings

1. Click **Credits** at the bottom of the left sidebar.
2. Click **Billing details**.

   ![The Credits menu with Billing details](https://git.law/kb-images/how-do-i-cancel-or-change-my-plan/credits-menu.png "narrow; ring: 3 59 94 19")

## Upgrading your plan

On the Billing page, click **View Plans** and select the plan you want.

![The plan card on the Billing page with View Plans](https://git.law/kb-images/how-do-i-cancel-or-change-my-plan/billing.png "ring: 89.4 28 9.4 44")

The upgrade takes effect immediately and you'll receive a higher monthly AI credit allowance from that point forward.

## Downgrading or cancelling

On the Billing page, click **View Plans** to choose a lower plan, or **Cancel plan** on your plan card to cancel. Before you confirm, GitLaw offers to **pause** your plan instead: pausing stops charges and freezes your credit balance until the plan resumes.

When you cancel, your plan runs to the end of the period you paid for. Then you move to the [free plan's](/knowledge-base/is-gitlaw-free) monthly AI credit allowance ($5/month), and unused plan credits are lost. Your account and all your documents are kept.

Your documents are never deleted when you downgrade or cancel. See [What happens to my documents if I cancel or delete my account?](/knowledge-base/what-happens-to-my-documents-if-i-cancel-or-delete-my-account) for more detail.

## Frequently asked questions

#### Will I lose my documents if I cancel?

No. Your account and documents remain intact. You move to the free plan, which gives you $5 of AI credits every month and full access to GitLaw's features.

#### What happens to my AI credits when I change plan?

Your monthly credit allowance adjusts to match your new plan. Unused credits from the current billing cycle do not roll over regardless of plan - see [How does billing work?](/knowledge-base/how-does-billing-work) for details on how credits work.

#### Can I re-upgrade later?

Yes. You can upgrade again at any time from your billing settings.
